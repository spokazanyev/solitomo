# Contract: Competitor Analysis Output

Дата: 2026-05-14.

## Required Output File

`07-build-specifications/competitor-marketing-analysis.md`

## Required Sections

1. Executive summary.
2. Competitor source table.
3. Global benchmark observations.
4. Russian/local supplier observations.
5. Marketing patterns.
6. Visual and UX patterns.
7. Product-detail page implications.
8. Category and SEO landing implications.
9. Homepage and B2B/RFQ implications.
10. Copywriting implications.
11. Claim control table.
12. Recommendations for next SDD features.
13. Source links.

## Minimum Counts

- At least 8 reviewed sources.
- At least 20 patterns.
- At least 15 Soliton recommendations.
- At least 1 claim-control table.

## Quality Rules

- Do not copy competitor text verbatim beyond short named terms.
- Every source observation must include a source link.
- Every recommendation must identify target page type.
- Every factual claim must have proof status.
- Recommendations must be usable by later specs for copywriting, design, page templates, and SEO.
