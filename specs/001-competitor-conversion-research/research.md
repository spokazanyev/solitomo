# Research: Competitor Conversion Research

Дата: 2026-05-14.

## Decision: Use A Mixed Global And Local Benchmark Set

The research will compare global PDU manufacturers and Russian/local suppliers.

Global benchmark sources:

- APC / Schneider Electric
- Raritan
- Vertiv / Geist
- Eaton / Tripp Lite
- Legrand / Server Technology

Russian/local sources:

- Hyperline
- DKC
- ЦМО / ITK or another relevant local supplier if public pages provide enough detail.

## Rationale

Global manufacturers show stronger product storytelling, technical proof, and enterprise conversion patterns. Russian suppliers show local catalog conventions, terminology, buyer expectations, and likely competitor presentation in search results.

Soliton needs both:

- modern high-trust technical marketing;
- local Russian buying expectations, logistics, payment, and B2B procurement language.

## Decision: Separate Observations From Recommendations

Every finding must be classified as:

- observed competitor pattern;
- Soliton recommendation;
- proof requirement;
- page-type implication.

## Rationale

This prevents unsupported claims from becoming published copy. It also lets later SDD features use the research as structured input for copywriting, design, page templates, and SEO.

## Decision: Prioritize Page-Level Conversion Patterns

The research will not only list features. It will identify how pages convert users:

- what the first screen says;
- how product families are grouped;
- how technical advantages are framed;
- how CTAs are placed;
- how datasheets and documents are surfaced;
- how engineering/procurement buyers are supported.

## Alternatives Considered

### Only Analyze Russian Competitors

Rejected because Russian ecommerce pages are often structurally useful but visually and copy-wise weaker than global manufacturer pages.

### Only Analyze Global Manufacturers

Rejected because Soliton sells in the Russian market and needs local B2B, delivery, payment, terminology, and procurement fit.

### Start With Design Before Competitor Research

Rejected because the project owner explicitly requires strong visual design and copywriting grounded in market patterns. Design should be informed by research.
