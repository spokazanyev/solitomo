# Contract: Copywriting And Positioning Output

Дата: 2026-05-14.

## Required Output File

`07-build-specifications/copywriting-and-positioning-spec.md`

## Required Sections

1. Executive summary.
2. Soliton positioning statement.
3. Audience segments.
4. Message pillars.
5. Claim-control matrix.
6. Tone of voice.
7. Banned and discouraged copy.
8. SEO and intent rules.
9. Page-level copy templates.
10. CTA library.
11. Technical copy rules.
12. First-release content priorities.
13. Inputs for next SDD features.

## Minimum Counts

- At least 5 audience segments.
- At least 7 page-level copy templates.
- At least 25 message or CTA patterns.
- Claim-control entries for all user-provided high-value claims.

## Quality Rules

- Copy must be technically specific.
- Copy must support SEO intent without keyword stuffing.
- Unsupported claims must have safer alternative wording.
- Comparative claims must not be used unless evidence and legal comfort exist.
- B2B/RFQ conversion must appear in every relevant page type.
