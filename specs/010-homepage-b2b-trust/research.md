# Research: Homepage B2B Trust

## Decisions

### Real Product Visuals

Use product images from the existing Soliton assortment on the homepage.

**Reasoning**: A technical ecommerce homepage needs visible product evidence. Existing product images are more useful than abstract decoration.

### Manufacturer Proof

Promote the core trust story: 15-year history, Russian production, registry, reliability, B2B documents.

**Reasoning**: The user identified these as important differentiators. They are especially relevant for corporate buyers and procurement contexts.

### B2B First Conversion

Make RFQ and project inputs more prominent than direct cart behavior.

**Reasoning**: PDU and rack power purchases often require quantity, city, timing, documents, and technical constraints. Direct online checkout can come later.

### Claim Control

Keep trust claims general and route specific proof to document/company sections.

**Reasoning**: This preserves conversion value while avoiding unsupported SKU-level promises.
